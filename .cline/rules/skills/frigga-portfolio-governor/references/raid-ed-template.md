## Status definitions

- **Green:** controlled; no material action required now.
- **Yellow:** attention or a near-term decision/action is required.
- **Red:** material threat, issue or blocked decision; escalation is required.
- **Uncertain:** insufficient, stale or conflicting evidence.

## Review prompts

- Which Yellow or Red entries need an owner decision now?
- Which assumptions must be verified before the next Gate?
- Which dependencies have no accountable owner or due point?
- Which evidence gaps make the current recommendation unreliable?
- Which entries may be closed, downgraded or escalated?
